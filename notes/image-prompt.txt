META PROMPT — FIGURES FOR BLOGS AND THOUGHTS
=============================================

Use this to make the image for any new blog post, notebook or thought, so it
matches the ones already on the site.

FORMAT (both kinds)
- 1200 x 630 canvas, rendered at 2x, exported as JPEG (quality ~82, 1600 px wide).
- Paper: #f0e9de with a faint warm grain. Ink: #151412. Secondary ink: #46433e.
  Muted: #85817a. Rules: #ddd3c1. One accent only: #e5461c (registration orange).
- Type: IBM Plex Mono for every label, small (10–12 px), uppercase, tracked.
  Newsreader only if a serif is unavoidable. No other fonts. No big titles.
- Top-left label:  "Fig. — <what it shows>"      (blogs)
                   "Notebook — <notebook name>"  (notebooks)
                   "Thoughts — <essay title>"    (thoughts)
- Bottom-left: the source credit, honest and specific (author, work, year,
  collection). Bottom-right: optional, one key fact in mono.
- Margins: 48 px. Nothing touches the edge. No logos, no faces of the author,
  no stock photos, no AI-generated pictures, no paintings in colour.

SOURCES
- Prefer an existing public-domain drawing, engraving, woodcut, plate or
  patent drawing (Wikimedia Commons, The Met, NGA, Wellcome, Royal
  Collection). Leonardo, Dürer, Rembrandt, Vesalius, Cajal, Gray, Galton,
  Muybridge, Darwin, Hogarth, Palladio, Flammarion are all in use already.
- Check the licence and write the credit. Never invent provenance.
- Lift the scan into the paper: grayscale, slight sepia (~0.35–0.4),
  contrast ~1.35, brightness ~1.1, then multiply onto the paper so the
  original paper disappears and only the ink stays.

BLOGS AND NOTEBOOKS (technical)
- The image must be accurate to the post, not decorative. Either:
  a) a historical drawing of the exact subject, annotated in accent orange
     (arrows, a box, a label: e.g. Gray's brain with the dorsal and ventral
     streams, Muybridge's frames marked for temporal and spatial attention), or
  b) a diagram computed from real numbers (e.g. the exact Binomial PMF, the
     binormal ROC curve and its AUC, k-means + Voronoi cells + true k-NN,
     the causal attention mask with exact 1/(i+1) rows).
- Every number shown must be computed, not typed in. Label what is
  illustrative versus exact.
- Orange marks only what matters: the answer, the stream, the true pair.

THOUGHTS (personal essays)
- One public-domain drawing or print, chosen for what the essay is about,
  not literally illustrating it (Melencolia for reflection, Praying Hands
  for asking for help, the Flammarion engraving for belief, Dürer's old man
  for a grandfather, Leonardo's heart for love).
- Centred as a plate, fitted inside 1040 x 470, no annotation, no accent
  colour on the drawing itself. The essay title sits small, top-left.

CHECKLIST BEFORE SAVING
- Does it look like a page from the same notebook as the others?
- Is every label true, and the credit complete?
- Would it still read at 400 px wide (the share preview)?
- Upload to Cloudinary (cloud dpq4fqlde) as site/blog/<slug>, site/blog/notebook-<slug>
  or site/thoughts/<slug>, and add the path to src/data/media.json; the post layouts pick it up by slug
  for the page header and the share image.
